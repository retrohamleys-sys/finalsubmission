About TUC Fencing Club

The Toulouse University Club Fencing Section welcomes students, university staff, and sports enthusiasts who want to discover one of the oldest Olympic sports. Our club promotes technical development, fair play, respect, and teamwork through regular training sessions and competitions.

Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced fencer, our coaches help members improve their skills in a supportive and dynamic environment.

Our Activities

Weekly Training

Professional coaching sessions designed for beginners and competitive athletes.

Competitions

Participate in local and regional tournaments while representing Toulouse University Club.

Student Community

Meet students from different backgrounds and build friendships through sport and teamwork.

Our goal

The fencing section of Toulouse University Club has introduced generations of students to the values of discipline, strategy, and sportsmanship. Through training, competition, and community events, the club continues to promote fencing within the university environment. Check our history in the specific section.
