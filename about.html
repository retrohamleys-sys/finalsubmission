About TUC Fencing Club

A tradition of excellence, discipline and sportsmanship in Toulouse.

Our History

The Toulouse University Club Fencing Section has introduced generations of students and sports enthusiasts to the art of fencing. Over the years, the club has become an important place for learning, competition, and personal development.

Through regular training sessions and participation in regional competitions, members have the opportunity to improve both their technical abilities and their confidence.

Our Values

Respect

Fencing teaches respect for opponents, coaches, teammates, and the rules of the sport.

Discipline

Success in fencing requires concentration, perseverance, and continuous improvement.

Team Spirit

Members support one another during training, competitions, and club activities.

Become a Member

Whether you are discovering fencing for the first time or already have experience, the TUC Fencing Club welcomes new members every year.
